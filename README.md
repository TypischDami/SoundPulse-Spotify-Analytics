## ✨ Key Features

- 🌌 **Cinematic Intro Animation** – Smooth intro sequence with glassmorphism UI.
- 📊 **Deep Listening Analytics** – Visualize your top artists, tracks, and total time listened in hours & days.
- 📅 **Detailed Monthly & Weekly Drilldown** – Filter your stats by custom time ranges down to individual weeks.
- 🎤 **Interactive Artist Deep-Dive** – Click on any rapper or artist to see your top tracks from them.
- 🗂️ **Local Data Processing** – Drag & drop your official Spotify JSON files safely. Everything stays 100% in your browser.
- 🔊 **Secret Twizzy Mode** – Try unlocking the hidden easter egg! 🔔

## 📖 How to Use

1. Request your **Extended Streaming History** from Spotify (Account Settings $\rightarrow$ Data Privacy).
2. Download the JSON files sent by Spotify.
3. Open [SoundPulse Analytics](https://typischdami.github.io/SoundPulse-Spotify-Analytics/).
4. Drag and drop your `.json` files into the upload box.
5. Explore your personal stats!
