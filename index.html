How do I get my Spotify JSON files?

  1. Visit spotify.com/account/privacy.
  2. Scroll down to "Download your data".
  3. Request the "Extended streaming history" (Spotify will send it via email).
  4. Unzip the downloaded folder and drag the `.json` files directly into this dashboard!
